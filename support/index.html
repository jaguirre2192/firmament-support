Support

A hand with your island

The villagers aren’t thinking

Thoughts and speech need an Apple Intelligence compatible device, Apple Intelligence enabled in Settings, and Apple’s on-device model ready to use. Some devices, languages, or regions may not support it. Firmament displays the model’s current availability.

If the model is getting ready, let the system finish preparing it. The island can still run while thoughts are unavailable.

Thoughts arrive slowly

On-device thinking happens one villager at a time. At faster simulation speeds, more island time can pass between thoughts. Try returning to 1× to give villagers more time to think.

Saving and starting over

Firmament has one saved island on this device. It saves automatically and offers a save action in the time bar’s menu. Keep the app open while a save or a long time skip finishes.

New island replaces the current island and its records after you confirm. There is no account or cloud sync to restore an island on another device. Removing the app may remove its local save.

Looking back through the Chronicle

The Chronicle’s day control lets you read records through an earlier day. This does not move the island or its people back in time. Field Notes show recorded changes in population, resources, and relationships.

Something seems wrong

The villagers and their words are fictional. Generated thoughts can be unexpected or mistaken. To report a problem, include the app version, device model, OS version, and the steps that led to it. A screenshot can help.

Contact support on GitHub

Support requests on GitHub are public. Please leave out personal information, account details, and anything you do not want others to see.
