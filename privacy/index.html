Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Firmament does not collect data from the app. It has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Its island simulation and villager thoughts run on your device.

What stays on your device

The app stores your island, its people, their fictional thoughts and speech, the Chronicle, Field Notes, and local simulation records in the app’s storage. Firmament does not upload these records to the developer or a cloud model.

On supported devices, villager thoughts use Apple’s on-device language model through Foundation Models. Firmament’s release version does not use a paid cloud model or ask you for an API key.

Your controls

You can replace the saved island using New island in the app, after confirming. Removing Firmament removes its app storage according to the operating system’s normal behavior. Device backups, diagnostics, purchases, and system services are managed by Apple under its policies and your device settings.

Support and this website

If you choose to contact support on GitHub, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be publicly visible. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in a support request.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts added by Firmament. GitHub may process technical information to provide its hosting service under the GitHub privacy statement.

Questions and changes

Jorge Aguirre develops Firmament. For questions about this policy, contact support. If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated.
